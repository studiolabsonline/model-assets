## Why the date matters

3DBenchy was released in 2015 under **CC BY-ND**, which forbids derivatives. It moved to
**CC0 / public domain in February 2025**, in a change made jointly by NTI, Daniel Norée,
and Paulo Kiefe, after the January 2025 enforcement episode on Printables.

So "3DBenchy is CC0" is only true with a date attached. Anything derived from a copy
retrieved before February 2025 sits under the old terms. Record the retrieval date for
every model added here, not just the license name.

## What was and was not changed

The files in this directory are **unmodified upstream bytes**, including their original
filenames, which contain `#`, spaces, and parentheses. They are kept verbatim so anyone can
diff them against upstream. Do not rename them.

Everything in `../dist/` is derived from these by
`3mf-quickview/docs/branding/generators/gen-benchy-models.py`.
